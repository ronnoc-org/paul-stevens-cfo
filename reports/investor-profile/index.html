Luap Chips Ltd

Independent distributor of electronic components. We buy chips in bulk reels and trays from manufacturers and distributors in Asia, the US and the UK, and supply break-packs to UK industrial and technology manufacturers. 01 · Company profile

A self-funded UK chip distributor with seven years of steady growth

Founded in January 2019, Luap Chips has grown into an £11m-revenue business operating from its own warehouse in Basingstoke, Hampshire. Revenue rose 9.2% in FY2025, and the first nine months of FY2026 are 8.7% ahead of the same period last year.

What we do

Manufacturers typically sell components in full reels and trays of thousands of units. Many UK manufacturers need smaller quantities, delivered quickly and with reliable traceability. Luap Chips buys in bulk, inspects every delivery at goods-in, holds stock in its own ESD-controlled warehouse and sells in smaller packs.

Our range covers 91 product lines across seven component categories: microcontrollers, memory, processors and SoCs, power management ICs, analogue and interface, sensors, and FPGAs and programmable logic.

We buy from twelve component suppliers: eight invoicing in US dollars (in China, Taiwan, Malaysia, South Korea, Hong Kong, Japan and the United States) and four UK distributors invoicing in sterling. About 80% of stock purchases are in US dollars, paid from our own USD account.

Our customers are 52 UK corporate manufacturers in nine industry segments. Five key accounts trade on 60-day terms and the rest on 30 days. We deliver with our own fleet.

Why the US

We already source from two US component suppliers and run most of our purchasing in US dollars. Expanding sales into the US would give us dollar revenue to match our dollar costs and widen a customer base that is currently all in the UK. This page supports our first conversations with potential investors and customers about that expansion.

03 · Capital structure

Founder-controlled, venture-backed and lightly geared

Issued share capital is 100,000 ordinary shares of £1 each, fully paid. The founder holds half the shares and Harbourline Capital Partners, our original investor, holds 40%. Bank borrowing is a reducing term loan, with a £1m revolving facility that was undrawn at 30 September 2026.

Shareholders

Revolving credit

Security

Tested quarterly: net debt to EBITDA no more than 2.5x; interest cover at least 4.0x; net assets at least £1.0m.

Interest cover uses the FY2025 full year. Net debt is borrowings less cash; a net cash position meets the leverage test with full headroom.

Investor protections

Under the 2019 investment agreement Harbourline has one board seat, monthly information rights and consent over reserved matters. These include new borrowing above £250,000, issuing shares, buying or selling a business and changes to the senior team. Any new equity for the US expansion would need Harbourline's consent.
